## What is KYC

KYC means Know Your Customer. It is the process through which regulated financial institutions identify and verify their customers as part of their customer due-diligence responsibilities. KYC helps financial institutions understand who their customers are and supports measures against misuse of the financial system.

## Customer identification

Banks and other regulated entities need appropriate information and documents to establish a customer's identity and, where applicable, address and other relevant details. The exact requirements depend on the type of customer, account and applicable regulatory rules. Customers should provide genuine and valid information during account opening and KYC procedures.

## KYC documents

Depending on the applicable requirements, customers may need officially accepted identity or address documents and other information. PAN or an equivalent document, where required, may also form part of the KYC process. The documents required can vary according to the customer's circumstances and the type of financial relationship.

## CKYC

Central KYC, commonly called CKYC, allows KYC information to be maintained in a centralized framework. A customer may have a KYC Identifier that can help regulated entities retrieve KYC records with the required consent and subject to applicable rules. This can reduce the need to repeatedly submit the same information in some situations.

## Periodic KYC updation

KYC information may need to be updated periodically according to the customer's risk category and applicable RBI requirements. Customers should respond to legitimate KYC-update requests through verified banking channels. A message asking customers to disclose confidential credentials or click an unknown link should not automatically be treated as a genuine KYC request.

## KYC fraud awareness

Fraudsters often use fake KYC-update messages to obtain OTPs, passwords, card information or access to bank accounts. Customers should verify KYC communications through the bank's official website, branch or customer-care channel. Never share a UPI PIN, card PIN, password or OTP with an unknown person claiming to complete KYC.
