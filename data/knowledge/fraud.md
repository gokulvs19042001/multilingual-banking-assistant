## Never share banking credentials

Banks and legitimate financial institutions do not require customers to disclose confidential credentials such as OTPs, PINs, CVVs or passwords to unknown callers. Treat unsolicited requests for these details as suspicious. Never enter or share such information because doing so can authorize or facilitate unauthorized transactions.

## Beware of phishing links

Fraudsters may send links through SMS, email, social media or messaging applications and pretend to represent a bank or financial service. A link may lead to a fake website designed to capture login credentials or payment information. Avoid opening unknown links and access banking services through the bank's official website or application.

## Fake customer-care scams

Scammers may publish or share fake customer-care numbers and then pretend to be bank representatives. They may ask for remote-access permissions, OTPs, card details or other confidential information. Use contact details published on the official bank website, banking application or other trusted official communication.

## UPI and payment fraud

A person receiving money generally does not need to enter a UPI PIN to receive a payment. A UPI PIN is used to authorize transactions from the linked bank account. Carefully check the transaction details before approving any payment request, QR-code payment or collect request.

## Lost card and unauthorized transactions

If a debit, credit or prepaid card is lost or stolen, it should be blocked immediately through the bank's official channel. Customers should also monitor transaction alerts and report unauthorized transactions to the bank without delay. Keeping mobile-number and email alerts active can help detect suspicious activity quickly.

## Reporting cyber financial fraud

If you become a victim of online financial fraud, report the incident as quickly as possible through the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal or the national cybercrime helpline 1930. Keep transaction references, screenshots, messages and other evidence available for the complaint. Prompt reporting can help authorities and financial institutions take action to restrict the movement of fraudulent funds.
