## Savings account

A savings account is a deposit account generally used by individuals to keep money safely while accessing routine banking services. Depending on the account and bank, customers may receive interest on eligible balances and access services such as transfers, cards and digital banking. Account terms, fees and interest rates vary by institution.

## Current account

A current account is generally designed for frequent business-related transactions. It commonly supports higher transaction activity and business payment requirements. The specific features, minimum-balance requirements and charges depend on the bank and account type.

## Interest

Interest is the amount paid or earned for the use of money over a specified period. A depositor may earn interest on eligible deposits, while a borrower generally pays interest on outstanding credit. The rate and calculation method depend on the financial product and its terms.

## EMI

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Instalment. It is a periodic payment used for many loans and generally contains a combination of principal repayment and interest. The actual EMI depends on factors such as loan amount, interest rate and repayment tenure.

## IFSC and MICR

IFSC is the Indian Financial System Code used to identify a specific bank branch for electronic fund transfers. MICR refers to Magnetic Ink Character Recognition and is associated with processing certain cheque transactions. Customers should use the codes exactly as provided by their bank or official banking records.

## NEFT, RTGS and IMPS

NEFT, RTGS and IMPS are electronic payment systems used for transferring money through the banking system. Their processing characteristics, transaction limits and settlement mechanisms can differ. Customers should select the appropriate method based on the transaction requirement and verify the beneficiary details before transferring funds.
