## Internet banking

Internet banking allows customers to access banking services through a bank's authorized website. Depending on the bank, customers may be able to view accounts, transfer funds, pay bills and manage selected services online. Customers should access internet banking only through the bank's verified website and protect their login credentials.

## Mobile banking

Mobile banking allows customers to perform banking activities through an authorized mobile application. Services can include balance enquiries, fund transfers, bill payments and account management. Customers should install banking applications from trusted sources and keep the application and mobile operating system updated.

## ATM banking

Automated Teller Machines, or ATMs, allow customers to perform services such as cash withdrawals, balance enquiries and other permitted transactions. Customers should protect their PIN while entering it and avoid accepting assistance from unknown persons. They should also collect their card and transaction receipt where applicable before leaving the ATM.

## Digital payment security

Customers should never share OTPs, passwords, UPI PINs, card PINs or CVVs with unknown persons. Banks and legitimate financial institutions generally do not require customers to disclose such confidential authentication information to complete routine transactions. Suspicious links, unexpected payment requests and unsolicited remote-access requests should be treated carefully.

## QR codes and online payments

QR codes can be used to initiate digital payments through supported payment applications. Customers should verify the displayed recipient name and amount before authorizing the transaction. Scanning a QR code does not mean that money will automatically be received; customers should understand what transaction the QR code is initiating.

## Reporting digital banking fraud

If an unauthorized digital transaction occurs, the customer should immediately contact the concerned bank or payment provider through its official channel. For financial cyber fraud, the incident should also be reported promptly through the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal or the helpline 1930. Customers should preserve transaction references, screenshots, messages and other evidence for investigation.
