## Savings account

A savings account is a bank deposit account commonly used by individuals for keeping money and carrying out regular transactions. Depending on the account type, the bank may pay interest on eligible balances. Customers can generally use services such as withdrawals, deposits, fund transfers and digital banking.

## Current account

A current account is primarily intended for customers who need frequent transactions, particularly businesses and other organizations. It generally supports regular receipts and payments without being designed primarily for savings. Minimum-balance requirements, transaction limits and charges depend on the bank and account variant.

## Basic Savings Bank Deposit Account

A Basic Savings Bank Deposit Account, or BSBDA, is designed to provide basic banking access with specified minimum features. Eligible customers can use the account for deposits, withdrawals and other permitted banking services. Banks must follow applicable RBI requirements regarding the facilities and conditions of such accounts.

## Salary account

A salary account is generally opened by an employer arrangement for receiving an employee's salary electronically. The exact benefits depend on the bank and the employer relationship. If salary credits stop, the account may be subject to the bank's applicable rules for its subsequent operation or conversion.

## Joint account

A joint account is an account held by two or more individuals according to an agreed operating mandate. The account can have different operating instructions, such as operation by all holders jointly or by specified holders individually. Customers should understand the mandate and responsibilities before opening a joint account.

## Nomination and account safety

Customers can generally provide a nominee for eligible deposit accounts so that the bank has instructions regarding the person entitled to receive the deposit proceeds after the customer's death, subject to applicable law and banking procedures. Customers should keep nominee and contact information updated. They should also protect account credentials and regularly monitor account transactions.
