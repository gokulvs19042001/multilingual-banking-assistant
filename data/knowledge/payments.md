## NEFT

National Electronic Funds Transfer, or NEFT, is an electronic funds transfer system operated under the Reserve Bank of India's framework. It allows customers to transfer money between participating bank accounts using the required beneficiary details. Customers should verify the account number, IFSC and beneficiary information before initiating a transfer.

## RTGS

Real Time Gross Settlement, or RTGS, is a payment system in which transactions are settled individually on a real-time basis. It is commonly used for transactions where immediate settlement is required and is subject to the applicable RBI rules and minimum-value requirements. Customers should verify transaction details carefully before authorizing an RTGS transfer.

## IMPS

Immediate Payment Service, or IMPS, enables customers to transfer money electronically through participating banks and payment channels. It is designed for near-real-time fund transfers and can be available beyond traditional banking hours, subject to the applicable service conditions. Transaction limits and charges depend on the relevant bank and payment channel.

## UPI

Unified Payments Interface, or UPI, enables customers to make instant account-to-account payments through participating applications. Users can transfer money using identifiers such as a UPI ID, mobile number or supported QR code. A UPI PIN is used to authorize transactions from the linked account and should never be shared.

## Choosing a payment method

NEFT, RTGS, IMPS and UPI serve different transaction requirements and have different processing characteristics. The appropriate method depends on factors such as urgency, transaction value, beneficiary details, availability and applicable limits. Customers should check the latest bank and payment-system rules before making an important transfer.

## Failed or disputed payments

A payment may sometimes show as pending, failed, reversed or disputed because of banking, network or system conditions. Customers should retain the transaction reference number and check the status through the relevant bank or payment application. If the issue is not resolved, the customer should use the applicable grievance process of the bank or payment service.
