## What is UPI

Unified Payments Interface, or UPI, is an instant payment system developed by NPCI that enables money transfers between bank accounts. It allows customers to use a mobile application and a UPI address instead of repeatedly entering bank-account details. UPI transactions can be performed around the clock, subject to the applicable bank and system conditions.

## UPI PIN

The UPI PIN is a confidential 4-6 digit authentication credential used to authorize UPI transactions. It should never be shared with another person, including someone claiming to be a bank employee or customer-care representative. A bank or legitimate support representative should not ask customers to disclose their UPI PIN.

## Sending money through UPI

To send money, the customer selects the recipient, verifies the payment information and authorizes the transaction using the UPI PIN. Before confirming, the payer should check the recipient name and transaction amount carefully. Once a payment is initiated, it generally cannot simply be cancelled by the payer through UPI.

## Receiving money through UPI

Receiving money does not normally require entering a UPI PIN. A person asking you to enter your UPI PIN to receive a refund, prize or payment may be attempting to make you authorize a debit. Always understand whether the transaction shown on the screen is a payment request or a request to receive money.

## UPI collect requests

A collect request asks the payer to approve a payment request through the UPI application. The payer should review who initiated the request and the amount before approving it. If the request is unexpected or suspicious, it should not be approved.

## UPI complaints and failed transactions

Customers can raise transaction-related complaints through the UPI application or participating bank according to the applicable grievance process. A transaction may sometimes show a delay or different status because of banking or network issues. Keep the transaction reference number and contact the bank or payment provider when a problem occurs.
