## Pradhan Mantri Jan-Dhan Yojana

Pradhan Mantri Jan-Dhan Yojana, or PMJDY, is a national financial-inclusion programme designed to expand access to formal banking services. It focuses on providing access to basic bank accounts and connecting eligible customers with services such as remittances, credit, insurance and pension. PMJDY accounts can also support the delivery of eligible government benefits through Direct Benefit Transfer.

## PMJJBY

Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana, or PMJJBY, is a government-backed life insurance scheme available through participating financial institutions subject to its eligibility and enrolment conditions. The scheme provides life insurance coverage for eligible subscribers according to the applicable rules. Customers should check the current premium, eligibility, coverage period and claim conditions from the official scheme information before enrolling.

## PMSBY

Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana, or PMSBY, is a government-backed personal accident insurance scheme. It provides specified accident-related insurance benefits to eligible subscribers subject to the scheme's terms and conditions. Customers should understand the eligibility requirements, premium, coverage and exclusions before joining.

## MUDRA loans

Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana supports eligible micro and small business activities through institutional credit. The scheme is intended to improve access to finance for micro enterprises and business activities that meet the applicable lending criteria. Loan approval, amount and repayment terms remain subject to the lender's assessment and the relevant scheme guidelines.

## PM SVANidhi

PM SVANidhi is a Central Sector Scheme designed to support eligible street vendors with working-capital credit. The scheme also encourages regular repayment and digital transaction adoption through applicable incentives. Eligibility, loan tranches and benefits are governed by the current scheme guidelines and should be checked through official channels.

## Choosing government financial schemes

Government financial schemes serve different purposes, such as banking access, insurance, pensions, business credit and livelihood support. Eligibility and benefits vary between schemes, so customers should not assume that qualification for one scheme automatically means qualification for another. The safest approach is to verify the latest eligibility, benefits, contribution or premium and application procedure through the official government portal or participating institution.
