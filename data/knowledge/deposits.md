## Fixed deposit

A Fixed Deposit, or FD, is a deposit in which money is placed with a bank for a specified period at an agreed interest rate. The customer generally receives the principal and applicable interest according to the deposit terms at maturity. Interest rates, tenure options and premature-withdrawal conditions vary between banks.

## Recurring deposit

A Recurring Deposit, or RD, allows a customer to deposit a specified amount at regular intervals for a predetermined period. It can help customers build a savings amount gradually instead of depositing the entire amount at once. The applicable interest rate, instalment requirements and premature-closure rules depend on the bank's terms.

## Deposit maturity

Maturity is the date on which a deposit reaches the end of its agreed tenure. At maturity, the bank handles the deposit according to the customer's instructions and the product terms. Customers should check whether the deposit will be paid out, renewed or handled through another applicable instruction.

## Interest on deposits

Banks pay interest on eligible deposits according to the applicable product terms and interest-rate structure. The rate may depend on factors such as deposit type, tenure and customer category. Customers should check the current rate and applicable conditions before opening a deposit.

## Premature withdrawal

Premature withdrawal means closing or withdrawing a deposit before the agreed maturity date. Depending on the deposit product, the bank may apply conditions, reduced interest or other applicable charges. Customers should check the premature-closure rules before making the deposit.

## Deposit insurance

Eligible bank deposits are covered by deposit insurance subject to the applicable rules and limits of the Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation. Deposit insurance is intended to protect depositors against the failure of an insured bank within the prescribed coverage framework. Customers should refer to DICGC for the current coverage limit and applicable conditions.
