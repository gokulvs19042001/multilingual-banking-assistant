## Debit card

A debit card allows a customer to access funds available in a linked bank account for permitted transactions. It can be used at ATMs, point-of-sale terminals and supported online or digital channels. The transaction generally results in money being debited from the linked account.

## Credit card

A credit card provides access to a credit facility up to an approved limit. The cardholder can make eligible purchases and is required to repay the amount according to the card's billing and repayment terms. Interest and other charges may apply when applicable, particularly when the outstanding amount is not paid as required.

## Card PIN and CVV

A card PIN is a confidential authentication credential used for certain card transactions. The CVV or similar card-security value may be required for some online transactions. Customers should never disclose their PIN, CVV, OTP or other confidential authentication information to unknown persons.

## Contactless payments

Contactless cards can support payments by bringing the card close to a compatible payment terminal. Applicable transaction limits and authentication requirements are determined by the payment system and regulatory framework. Customers should verify the transaction amount before completing a contactless payment.

## Lost or stolen card

A lost or stolen card should be blocked immediately using the bank's official channel. Customers should also report any unauthorized transactions as soon as they are noticed. The bank may provide a replacement card according to its applicable procedures and terms.

## Safe card usage

Customers should never share card credentials through calls, messages, emails or social-media conversations with unknown persons. They should use trusted websites and applications for online purchases and monitor transaction alerts regularly. Card limits and controls offered by the bank can also be used to reduce exposure to unauthorized transactions.
